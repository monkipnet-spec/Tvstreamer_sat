TVStreammerSAT5 v202.6

HLS input / UDP pacing:
- Keep v202.5 continuous 20 ms CBR PCR and HLS-aware CC handling.
- Stop using short PCR byte-density as the HLS useful-data bitrate on VBR services.
- Derive HLS playout rate from bytes per GStreamer PTS/DTS media time over a 6 s rolling window.
- HTTP arrival bitrate remains ignored for HLS once media timestamps are available.
- PCR-derived bitrate remains diagnostic/startup fallback only.
- New stats: hls_pts_rate, hls_pts_samples, hls_pts_resets.

Reason:
Runtime logs showed no underflow, no missed PCR intervals and no CC errors, while hls_pcr_rate oscillated ~2.5-3.5 Mbit/s on a ~2.82 Mbit/s useful stream. That oscillation is expected for VBR byte density and was incorrectly driving the playout rate, causing periodic freezes and A/V drift.
