TVStreammerSAT5 Release 73 / v202.7

HLS -> UDP video freeze stabilization

Problem observed on v202.6:
- audio became stable;
- no UDP underflow, TS resync or CC verification errors;
- video still froze periodically;
- HLS real_pace followed the 6 s PTS bitrate estimate and moved by several percent
  (about 2.58..2.92 Mbit/s in the captured log) while the 20 ms CBR PCR clock
  remained continuous.

v202.7 changes:
- keep the v202.5/v202.6 HLS queue, CC handling and continuous 20 ms CBR PCR;
- replace direct 6-second PTS-rate chasing with a slow HLS playout PLL;
- lock a long-lived base useful-TS pace;
- update the PLL only every 5 seconds;
- long-term PTS rate changes enter the base at 1/32 per update;
- reservoir correction is limited to +/-1.5%;
- actual pace change is limited to 0.2% per 5-second update;
- HLS reservoir target is 8 seconds to absorb segment burstiness without
  modulating video packet spacing;
- add hls_pll_base and hls_pll_correction telemetry.

Expected log marker:
HLS UDP pacing v202.7: mode=slow-playout-pll ...

Expected stats marker:
timing=hls_slow_playout_pll_periodic_pcr
