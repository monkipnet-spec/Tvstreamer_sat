TVStreammerSAT5 202.46

Memory containment / RSS stabilization:
- Added hard byte limits to all long-lived GStreamer queues in addition to their time limits.
  This prevents MPEG-TS queues from retaining unbounded data when timestamps/durations are
  missing or discontinuous during network reconnect/remap.
- Network HLS/SRT/HTTP MPEG-TS input queues are capped at 20 MiB while retaining the 5 s
  latency window introduced in 202.45.
- Generic stream queues now receive a dynamic 8..32 MiB hard cap sized for approximately
  32 Mbit/s and their configured time window.
- UDP input queues are capped at 12 MiB for internal DVB loopback and 8 MiB for external live UDP.
- Transcoded SRT relay queues now use the same bounded generic queue helper.
- DVB scan queue receives an 8 MiB hard cap.
- On glibc systems malloc arenas are capped at 16 and the trim threshold is reduced to 512 KiB.
  This reduces RSS fragmentation in a process with hundreds of GStreamer/SRT worker threads.
- Pipeline teardown/rebuild paths perform a throttled malloc_trim(0) so freed queue/buffer pages
  are returned to the OS after reconnects and channel stops instead of remaining cached in arenas.

No protocol watchdog timing changes versus 202.45:
- generic no-input watchdog: 6 s
- HLS no-input watchdog: 15 s
- initial SRT startup allowance: 15 s
